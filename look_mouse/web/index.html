2. Pointer check

Click each screen in the diagram. If the cursor lands in the middle of the screen you clicked, this is set correctly. If it always stays on your primary screen, choose the second option.
Stepping distance: pixels per step.
To measure it, rest the mouse over the left of this box with clear space to its right on the same screen, then press M. The cursor will slide right.

Glasses camera (optional)

A small camera on your glasses, pointed at one eye, gives a much finer reading of eye direction. The desk camera still tracks your head. Calibrate again after switching this on or off, and whenever the glasses have shifted.

3. Calibrate

Move this browser window onto the screen you want to calibrate, choose it below and start. A dot appears full-screen: look at it and press Space. One point is enough to pick the screen; nine points lets the cursor land where you look.
